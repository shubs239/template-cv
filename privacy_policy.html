Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2025

1. Introduction

At Upgrade Me, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our CV creation service.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal data from users. All information entered during the CV creation process is used solely for generating your CV and is deleted immediately after the PDF is created and downloaded.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used exclusively to generate your CV. We do not use your data for marketing, analytics, or any other purpose. Once your CV is downloaded, all data is permanently erased from our systems.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data during transmission and processing. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with any third parties. Our service does not integrate with external databases, cloud storage, or analytics platforms.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the service after any such changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@Upgrade Me.com.
